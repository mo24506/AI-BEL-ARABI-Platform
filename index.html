FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is everything on the platform free?
Yes. All lectures, slides, datasets and notebooks are free to watch and download. No subscription, no hidden fees.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Just open a course and start. Your progress and personal notes are saved automatically in your browser on this device.
What background do I need?
Each course lists its prerequisites. For AI Meets Biotech Advanced you need basic molecular biology and introductory Python — and Module 0 helps you catch up on the Python part.
Do I need a powerful computer for the labs?
No. Labs run in Google Colab, which gives you free cloud computing in your browser.
What language are the lectures in?
Lectures are explained in Arabic. Scientific and technical terms stay in English so you can read research papers with confidence.
How do I hear about new lectures and courses?
New lectures appear in the course curriculum as soon as they are released. Follow our social channels (linked at the bottom of the page) for announcements.
